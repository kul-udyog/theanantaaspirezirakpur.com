About the Project

Built for the way Tricity families actually live

The Ananta Aspire sits directly on the Chandigarh-Patiala Highway (NH-7) in Zirakpur, a stretch that's become one of the busiest addresses in the Tricity for families who work in Chandigarh or Mohali but want more space for their money. Spread over 34,050 sq. yards, the project holds 440 apartments across 3, 3+1 and 4+1 BHK layouts, each block built with only two apartments per floor.

That two-per-floor layout is the detail most buyers notice on their first site visit — it means no shared walls with a stack of neighbours, less lift-lobby traffic, and homes that stay genuinely private. Add Mivan monolithic construction and a resort-style clubhouse and pool deck, and you get a build quality and lifestyle that shows up in person, not just in the brochure.

Every apartment is designed to catch cross-ventilation and daylight through the day, with double-glazed windows to cut highway noise and a water-softener plant feeding the whole project.

The Design Advantage

A dual-core layout, built for light and air on both sides

Most apartment layouts in the Tricity give you one open face and a stack of shared walls. The Ananta Aspire is designed around a dual-core plan — with only two apartments on every floor, each home opens on both sides, facing the landscaped park on one end and the wider skyline on the other.

In practice, that means cross-ventilation and daylight move through the apartment through the day rather than being trapped behind a single window wall, and every balcony looks out over green space rather than a neighbouring tower.

It's the detail that gives these apartments a villa-like feel despite being part of a 440-unit community — worth experiencing on a site visit rather than reading about.

Why Zirakpur

Investing in the Tricity's fastest-growing corridor

Connectivity

Sitting directly on NH-7 (Chandigarh-Patiala Highway), Zirakpur has become the default entry point into the Tricity from Delhi/Ambala, with the Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport, Chandigarh, roughly 15 minutes away.

Social Infrastructure

Elante Mall, Paras Downtown and Cosmo Plaza cover shopping and entertainment within 4–15 minutes, while Fortis Mohali and GMCH Sector 32 keep quality healthcare close by.

Appreciation Potential

As one of the last stretches of NH-7 still absorbing new supply, Zirakpur has continued to see steady end-user and investor demand as Chandigarh and Mohali itself run out of land.
